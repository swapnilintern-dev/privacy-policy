Privacy Policy

VS Arogya — Android app and website

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VS Arogya ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal information when you use the VS Arogya mobile application and website (together, the "Service").

VS Arogya is a business-to-business (B2B) platform for ordering medicines and healthcare products. It is meant for registered businesses in India, such as pharmacies and medical stores, wholesalers, hospitals and clinics, and for our own staff (marketing, delivery, outlet and agent roles). By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

1. Information we collect

a) Account and business information (you provide it)

  • Store / business name, contact person name, mobile number and email address
  • Business address, city, state and PIN code, and any delivery addresses you save
  • GST status and GST number, drug licence number and drug licence expiry date
  • Documents you upload for verification: GST certificate, drug licence copy and a store photo
  • Your password, if you set one. It is stored only in hashed (encrypted) form and we cannot read it.

b) Order and transaction information

  • Products, quantities, cart items, saved products, coupons used, order history and invoices
  • Payment status and payment reference IDs. We receive these from Razorpay.
  • Delivery details, including proof-of-delivery information recorded by our delivery staff

c) Payment information

Payments are processed by Razorpay. Your card, UPI or bank details are entered directly with Razorpay and are not stored on our servers. We only receive the payment ID, amount and status.

d) Login and technical information

  • One-time passwords (OTPs) that we send to your email for sign-in. They expire after a short time.
  • Your last login time and whether you have notifications turned on
  • A login token and your app preferences, stored on your own device so that you stay signed in
  • Basic server logs, such as IP address, request time and errors. Our hosting provider keeps these for security and troubleshooting.

e) Information we do NOT collect

We do not collect your precise location, contacts, call logs or SMS messages. We do not show third-party advertising, and the app does not include any advertising or analytics tracking SDKs. We only access photos or files when you choose to upload them, for example verification documents.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account, and to sign you in with an email OTP or password
  • To verify that your business is eligible to buy medicines. This includes checking your GST and drug licence.
  • To show you the products and prices that apply to your account type
  • To process orders, payments, invoices, deliveries and returns
  • To send service messages, such as OTPs, approval or rejection emails, order updates and in-app notifications
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, keep the Service secure, and meet our legal, tax and regulatory obligations

4. Data retention

  • Account information is kept while your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your profile, saved addresses, cart, saved products and login details are removed.
  • Orders, invoices and payment records are kept for as long as Indian tax (GST), accounting and drug-sale laws require, even after you delete your account.
  • Email OTPs are deleted automatically after they expire.

5. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Privacy & Security → Delete Account → Delete Permanently. Your account is deleted straight away.

Without the app: email vsarogyameda@gmail.com from your registered email address with the subject "Account deletion request". We will delete your account within 7 working days and confirm by email.

6. Security

All data between the app or website and our servers is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are stored as hashes. Access to data depends on each user's role, and sign-in OTPs expire after a short time and are limited to a few attempts. No method of storing or sending data is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Your rights

Under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can:

  • Ask for a copy or summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct or update your information. You can edit most details in your profile.
  • Ask us to delete your account and personal data (see Section 5)
  • Turn off notifications in the app
  • Raise a complaint with us (see Section 10)

8. Children

The Service is for businesses and adults aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will also notify you in the app or by email.
